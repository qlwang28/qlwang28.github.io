Student Recruitment

I hope to work with students who love life, stay optimistic, pursue growth, and share our goals.

Priority will be given to students aiming to publish at leading AI venues, pursue algorithm or R&D careers in industry, or pursue a Ph.D. and reach higher academic goals.

If you have a clear plan for your future, I would be glad to work with you and explore what we can achieve together.

In addition to recruiting students at Shenzhen Technology University, I welcome collaborators interested in exploring large language models and related topics together.

As an active researcher, I can provide close, practical guidance throughout the research process—from learning the basics and developing ideas to designing experiments, implementing and debugging code, deriving equations, and writing papers.
